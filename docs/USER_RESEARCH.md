# User research: FeedbackIQ

> **Status: research plan ready; interviews not yet run.** Add findings to section 5 after the interviews.

## 1. Goal

Learn how people who handle customer feedback do it today, and whether FeedbackIQ's ranked pain points, quotes and recommendations would change what they do.

## 2. Who to interview (3–5 people)

| Profile | Why |
|---|---|
| Someone in customer support / CX (any company) | Reads complaints daily |
| A product manager or analyst | Turns feedback into priorities |
| An ops or store manager (food, retail or delivery) | Acts on process problems such as missing items |
| A friend who tried the live app (tester) | First-use usability |

Avoid only interviewing people who will be polite. Tell them honest criticism is the most useful thing they can give.

## 3. Interview script (25 minutes)

**Intro (2 min)**
"I'm researching how teams handle customer feedback. There are no right answers, and I'm not testing you. Can I take notes?"

**Current process (8 min)**
1. Tell me about the last time you dealt with customer complaints. What happened, step by step?
2. Where does feedback come from, and how much per week?
3. How do you decide which problem to fix first?
4. What's the most frustrating part of that process?
5. When did a priority turn out to be wrong? What happened?

**Using FeedbackIQ (10 min)**: share the live app; they drive, you watch.
6. "Find the biggest problem customers have." *(observe: where do they click? what confuses them?)*
7. Would you trust this ranking? Why or why not?
8. What would make you share this report with your manager?
9. What's missing that would stop you from using this weekly?

**Wrap-up (5 min)**
10. If this disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss, if anything?
11. On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to use this in your job? What would make it a 10?
12. Who else should I talk to?

**Rules for the interviewer:** ask about past behaviour, not hypotheticals; don't explain or defend the product; ask "why?" after short answers; note exact quotes.
